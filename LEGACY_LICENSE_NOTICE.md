# Historical licensing notice

## Product-policy boundary

The intended product boundary was:

- through `v0.4.x`: **Arena Dev Community**, distributed under the MIT License;
- from `v0.5.0` onward: proprietary product development.

That policy was not applied correctly to the repository at the time.

## Releases actually distributed under MIT

The Git tags/releases `v0.5.0`, `v0.6.0`, `v0.7.0` and `v0.8.0` were published
with an MIT `LICENSE` file still present. Consequently, copies of those
historical releases that were obtained under the MIT License retain the rights
granted by that license. The current proprietary policy does not attempt to
revoke those historical grants.

This notice records the discrepancy instead of rewriting release history.

## Proprietary baseline

`v0.9.0` is the first release that is intentionally and explicitly distributed
under the **Arena Dev Proprietary License**.

Current and future versions are proprietary unless a specific file or release
states otherwise in writing.

## Third-party components

Open-source and other third-party dependencies remain governed by their own
licenses. The Arena Dev Proprietary License applies only to material for which
the Arena Dev copyright holder has the authority to set those terms.
